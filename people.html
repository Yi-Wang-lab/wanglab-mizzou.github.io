Dr. Yi Wang joined the University of Missouri as an Assistant Professor of Industrial and Systems Engineering in 2022. His research covers advanced micro/nanomanufacturing, multifield-assisted 3D printing for terrestrial and in-space manufacturing, biomedical devices such as flexible brain–machine interfaces, ultrasonic-assisted machining, and AI-driven smart manufacturing with mixed reality, IIoT, and digital twins.

Dr. Wang received his Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from North Carolina State University in 2022, with a dissertation on the design, fabrication, and implantation control of flexible neural microprobes for medical neurology applications. He received his M.Eng. in Instrument and Meter Engineering (2016) and his B.Eng. in Measuring and Control Technology and Instrumentations (2013) from Tianjin University, China.

He is a recipient of the NSF CAREER Award (2026) and was named Outstanding Professor in Industrial and Systems Engineering in Spring 2025. He serves as a Guest Editor for Bioengineering (MDPI) and a Young Editorial Board Member of the Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Science and Technology.
