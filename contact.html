Prospective graduate students

We look for students with backgrounds in industrial, mechanical, electrical, biomedical, or materials engineering, or a related field. Apply to the Industrial and Systems Engineering graduate program at the University of Missouri, and email Dr. Wang your CV, transcripts, and a short description of your research interests.

Undergraduate students

Mizzou undergraduates are welcome to get involved in research. Email Dr. Wang with your major, your year, and the projects that interest you.

Postdocs and visiting scholars

Please email Dr. Wang your CV and a brief research statement.
